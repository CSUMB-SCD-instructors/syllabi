# CST 696 (Graduate Directed Study) Syllabus

## Course Information

### Course Details

- **Course Title:** Graduate Directed Study
- **Course Number:** CST 696
- **Meeting Location:** NA
- **Units:** 1-4

### Course Description

Independent faculty-supervised study and research on advanced topics.

### Materials

- No required textbook

### Technology Requirements

- No technology requirements

### Course Objectives

Objectives will vary depending on the individual study, internship, or project topic. Students will work closely with faculty and/or external partners.

## Grading

Grading will be based on the learning goals established with the supervising faculty member. Evaluation may include research papers, project deliverables, presentations, and participation as appropriate to the agreed study plan.

## Syllabus Change Policy

This syllabus is subject to change at the instructor's discretion. Students will be notified of any substantive changes via Canvas announcement and/or email.
