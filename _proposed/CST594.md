# CST CST594 (Graduate Internship in Computer Science) Syllabus

## Course Information

### Course Details

- **Course Title:** Graduate Internship in Computer Science
- **Course Number:** CST CST594
- **Meeting Location:** Physical
- **Units:** 1-8

### Course Description

This course provides supervised professional experience in computer science or a related field that integrates graduate coursework with practical application. Students apply advanced computing knowledge and skills in a professional setting and connect their work experience to their academic and career goals through reflection, documentation, and other faculty-approved academic activities. The scope of the internship and associated academic requirements will reflect the number of units earned.

### Materials

No textbook is required. Students are responsible for any materials, software, accounts, training, or safety requirements specified by their internship site. Course readings, templates, and assignment instructions will be provided through Canvas as needed.

### Technology Requirements

Students must have access to:
- A computer (not "chromebook" or similar) with reliable internet connection
- Access to Canvas learning management system

### Course Objectives

At the end of this class, you should be able to:

1. Apply graduate-level computer science knowledge and technical skills to authentic professional work.
2. Communicate technical progress, challenges, and results effectively to both workplace and academic audiences.
3. Demonstrate professional practices, including collaboration, ethical conduct, time management, and responsiveness to feedback.
4. Critically reflect on the relationship between internship experiences, graduate coursework, and longer-term career goals.

## Grading

Grades are based on successful completion of the approved internship experience and associated academic work. Students are expected to complete the hours and responsibilities established in their internship agreement; communicate promptly with the faculty instructor and site supervisor; and submit required work through Canvas by the stated deadlines.

| Assignment Kind | Value |
|:----------------|:-----:|
| [Internship Performance](#internship-performance) | 40% |
| [Professional Documentation](#professional-documentation) | 30% |
| [Reflection and Faculty Check-ins](#reflection-and-faculty-check-ins) | 30% |

**Getting below a 40% in any of these three groups will result in failing the class.**

### Internship Performance

Internship performance is evaluated using evidence of satisfactory completion of agreed-upon responsibilities, professional conduct, and feedback from the site supervisor. The faculty instructor may request a supervisor evaluation or confirmation of completed work and hours.

### Professional Documentation

Students will maintain and submit documentation of their internship work. This may include a work plan, periodic activity or hours log, deliverables or work samples where permitted, and a final summary of accomplishments. Students must respect employer confidentiality requirements; confidential materials should not be submitted to Canvas without prior approval.

### Reflection and Faculty Check-ins

Students will complete short reflections and participate in scheduled check-ins with the faculty instructor. These activities connect workplace experience to computer science concepts, professional development, ethical considerations, and career planning.

## Syllabus Change Policy

This syllabus is subject to change at the instructor's discretion to enhance learning or accommodate unforeseen circumstances. Students will be notified of any substantive changes (such as changes to due dates, point values of assignments, or major policy modifications) via Canvas announcement and/or email at least one week in advance when possible.
