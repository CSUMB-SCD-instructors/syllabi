# CST 594 (Graduate Internship in Computer Science) Syllabus

## Course Information

### Course Details

- **Course Title:** Graduate Internship in Computer Science
- **Course Number:** CST 594
- **Meeting Location:** NA
- **Units:** 1-9

### Course Description

Internship participation at corporations, agencies, or non-profits with faculty and site supervision.

### Materials

- No required textbook

### Technology Requirements

- No technology requirements

### Course Objectives

Objectives will vary depending on the internship. Students will work closely with faculty and/or external partners.

## Grading

Evaluation will be based on deliverables defined in the internship.

## Syllabus Change Policy

This syllabus is subject to change at the instructor's discretion. Students will be notified of any substantive changes via Canvas announcement and/or email.
