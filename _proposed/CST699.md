# CST 699 (Graduate Capstone) Syllabus

## Course Information

### Course Details

- **Course Title:** Graduate Capstone
- **Course Number:** CST 699
- **Meeting Location:** Physical
- **Units:** 4

### Course Description

Completion and presentation of a culminating capstone project integrating program knowledge.

### Materials

- No required textbook

### Technology Requirements

Students must have access to:
- A computer with reliable internet connection
- Access to Canvas learning management system

### Course Objectives

Objectives will vary depending on the project topic. Students will work closely with faculty and/or external partners.

## Grading

Evaluation will be based on deliverables defined in the project.

## Syllabus Change Policy

This syllabus is subject to change at the instructor's discretion. Students will be notified of any substantive changes via Canvas announcement and/or email.
