# CST 695 (Graduate Special Topics in Computer Science) Syllabus

## Course Information

### Course Details

- **Course Title:** Graduate Special Topics in Computer Science
- **Course Number:** CST 695
- **Meeting Location:** Physical
- **Units:** 1-4

### Course Description

Selected advanced topics in computer science; content varies by semester.

### Materials

- No required textbook

### Technology Requirements

- No technology requirements

### Course Objectives

Learning objectives will vary by semester depending on the chosen topic. In general, students will gain depth in an advanced or emerging area of computer science and apply their knowledge through readings, discussion, and project-based work under faculty guidance.

## Grading

Grading will be determined by the instructor based on the specific course topic. Evaluation may include a mix of projects, presentations, written work, and participation as appropriate to the subject matter.

## Syllabus Change Policy

This syllabus is subject to change at the instructor's discretion. Students will be notified of any substantive changes via Canvas announcement and/or email.
