# CST 597 (Graduate Independent Study in Computer Science) Syllabus

## Course Information

### Course Details

- **Course Title:** Graduate Independent Study in Computer Science
- **Course Number:** CST 597
- **Meeting Location:** NA
- **Units:** 1-4

### Course Description

Research and/or project work under the supervision of a program faculty.

### Materials

- No required textbook

### Technology Requirements

Objectives will vary depending on the individual study, or project topic. Students will work closely with faculty and/or external partners.

Evaluation will be based on deliverables defined in the independent study, or project.
