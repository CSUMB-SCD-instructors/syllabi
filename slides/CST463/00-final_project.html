The Project

Baseline, then stretch

Baseline

The simplest reasonable thing that gives you a number you can trust

  • pretrained model + new head
  • or a small model from scratch
  • proper held-out split

Stretch

A question you answer with an experiment

  • frozen vs. fine-tuned?
  • how much data is enough?
  • what does it get wrong, and why?


A stretch is a question, not just “a bigger model”

Where do ideas come from?

  • A list of ideas is in final-project/project_ideas.md
    • images, text, audio, time series, games, multimodal
    • each with a dataset and a compute tier
  • Or bring your own, as long as you can already load the data


The dataset is the first thing that goes wrong, so check it first

Compute tiers

Tier What it means
A Free Colab or a laptop, minutes per run
B Wants a GPU, tens of minutes per run. Use a subset or a small model
C Too heavy. Shrink it


  • Time one batch or one epoch on a small subset before you commit
  • Start from pretrained models, and compare against training without one when it makes sense
  • Shrink aggressively: 10% of the data and a careful comparison beats a big run you can’t repeat

What Makes a Good Project

What I’m looking for

  • You did the sensible things first
  • Your claims are backed by evidence (a plot, a table)
  • You can explain why you made each choice, not just what
  • You looked at the failures, not just the accuracy
  • Someone else could reproduce your work


It’s the same thing I’ve been asking for in the homework reports
